<i>Vocabify</i> is an ad-free, offline-friendly dictionary for <a href='https://dictionaryapi.dev/'>Free Dictionary API</a> and <a href="https://wiktionary.org/">Wiktionary API</a>. Just type a word to look it up.

<b>Features</b>
<ol>
    <li>English to English dictionary</li>
    <li>Multi-language to English lookup using Wiktionary API (case-sensitive)</li>
    <li>Definitions, examples, synonyms/antonyms, and etymology where available</li>
    <li>Pronunciation: IPA text plus audio using TTS, and speech-to-text search input</li>
    <li>Search history and favourites, cached locally for offline reuse with an offline banner</li>
    <li>Flashcards learning mode built on your favourites</li>
    <li>Word of the day: daily notification plus home-screen widget</li>
    <li>Share-to-search from any app</li>
    <li>Quick Settings tile for the persistent overlay (bubble) search</li>
    <li>Backup and restore: export and import your history and favourites</li>
    <li>Random word discovery</li>
    <li>Material 3 / Material You with dynamic color, dark, light, and OLED themes</li>
    <li>Adaptive layouts for phones, tablets, and landscape</li>
</ol>

Vocabify is free and open source under GPL-3.0, based on freeDictionaryApp by Yamin Siahmargooei.
